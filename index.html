Common Questions
What domains do you specialise in? +
My deepest expertise is in healthcare UX — patient portals, RPM, e-pharmacy, and AI health tools. I also have strong experience in B2B SaaS dashboards, education platforms, and consumer mobile apps.
What tools do you use? +
Figma and Adobe XD for design and prototyping. Protopie for advanced micro-interactions. Marvel and Balsamiq for rapid wireframing. Dora for no-code web design.
Are you available for freelance work? +
Yes — I'm open to both freelance projects and full-time opportunities as stated on my Behance profile. Reach out at bediprashant143@gmail.com with your project brief.

About Me

UIX Designer.
Team Lead.
Photographer.

I'm Prashant Bedi — a UIX designer from Himachal Pradesh, currently based in Chandigarh. With 7+ years of experience across healthcare, AI, and consumer apps, I lead teams, craft design systems, and ship products people love to use.

Say hello → View Behance ↗

The story so far

My design journey started in 2018 at Parmax Web Studio in Mohali, where I designed everything from logos and brochures to full websites. I learned that great design is invisible — it just feels right.

At Ditstek Innovations (2020–2025), I grew into a Creative Team Lead — managing 6 designers, owning 50+ product launches, and building deep expertise in healthcare UX. I designed intuitive apps for doctors, patients, and caregivers.

Since May 2025 at ScryAI in Gurugram, I'm working at the frontier of AI-powered design — collaborating with data scientists to make complex AI interfaces feel simple and human.

Outside work, I shoot street photography under @prick.clicks on Instagram — looking for the moments people walk past.
